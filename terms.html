Terms of use

1. What F1zz Tweaks is

F1zz Tweaks is a Windows app that changes Windows settings that you choose. Nothing is applied until you switch it on or press an apply button.

2. Use it at your own risk

Changing system settings always carries some risk. We design every tweak to be reversible and the app saves your original values before it changes them, but we can't promise a tweak will work on every PC, or that it will make your PC faster. Results vary. We recommend creating a Windows restore point first (the app has a button for it). To the extent the law allows, F1zz Tweaks is provided "as is" and we are not responsible for lost data, lost time or damage that comes from using it.

3. Free and Premium

  • The free app is free to use for personal use.
  • Premium includes the Premium tweaks, the Premium tools (Tweak Guard, profiles and undo from the change history), unlimited F1zz Bot chats, the full PC report from F1zz Bot, the extra color themes and the extra Game Library games. Free users get the free tweaks and 5 F1zz Bot messages a day.
  • Tweak Guard only re-applies tweaks that F1zz Tweaks applied for you, and only while the app is open. You can switch it off at any time on the Premium page.
  • A Premium code gives one person access to the Premium tweaks on one PC. Codes are locked to that PC's Device ID. You may not share, resell or publish your code.
  • We may turn off a code that is shared, resold, refunded, charged back or used to abuse the service.
  • If you get a new PC, open a ticket and we will help you move your code.

Premium Plus and Premium Ultimate

  • Premium Plus and Premium Ultimate are lifetime Premium codes, under the same rules as other Premium codes. Plus also includes the PS5 Tweaks and Console Extras guides. Ultimate also includes those, plus the Xbox Tweaks and PC Pro guides. The 30-day and Lifetime plans don't include these guides.
  • You read the guides in the F1zz Console Tweaks app for Windows, on the same PC your code is locked to. The app doesn't connect to your console or change it for you, so you make each change yourself. The guides don't include mods or jailbreaks, and aren't made or endorsed by Microsoft, Sony or any TV, router or hardware maker. The PC Pro guide includes driver and BIOS settings, which carry risk, so follow its warnings and use it at your own risk. Results depend on your hardware and internet connection, and aren't guaranteed.
  • If you get a new PC, open a ticket and we'll move your code. You may not share, resell or publish your code or the guides. Your order ID shows in the guides, so a leaked copy can be traced to its order, and we may turn off a code that's shared, resold, published, refunded or charged back.

4. When Premium ends

When a Premium license expires, is refunded, is charged back or is removed, the app tells you and then automatically restores the Premium tweaks it applied back to your original settings. This happens the next time the app is opened and can check your license. The Premium color themes go back to the default theme, F1zz Bot goes back to 5 messages a day, and the Premium tools (Tweak Guard, profiles and undo from the history) switch off. Saved profiles and the change history stay on your PC. Your free tweaks, and any settings you had before using F1zz Tweaks, are not touched. A few tweaks need the app to be run as administrator to be restored.

Refund policy

The short version: if Premium doesn't work for you, open a ticket within 7 days of buying and tell us what happened. We'll try to fix it with you, and if we can't, we'll refund you.

Who can get a refund

  • You bought Premium (the 30-day or the lifetime plan) within the last 7 days.
  • Premium didn't work on your PC, or something went wrong that we couldn't fix with you in a ticket.
  • Your code has not been shared, resold or published.

What isn't covered

  • Requests made after 7 days, unless the law where you live says otherwise.
  • Codes that were shared, resold, published or used to abuse the service.
  • Premium working as described but not making your PC as fast as you hoped. Results vary by PC and aren't guaranteed.
  • Needing a code for a new PC. That isn't a refund. Open a ticket and we'll move your code (see below).

How to ask

Open a ticket in our Discord and send your order number and what happened. We reply as soon as we can, usually within 48 hours.

How you get your money back

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through our checkout provider. We never see your card details. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or card company, often a few business days.

What happens to Premium after a refund

  • Your Premium license ends. The app finds out when it is opened and checks online (and again every few hours while it runs). If your PC is offline, it happens as soon as it's online again.
  • The app tells you, then restores the Premium tweaks it applied back to your original settings (see "When Premium ends" above).
  • The Premium color themes go back to the default theme, F1zz Bot goes back to 5 messages a day, and the Premium tools switch off.
  • Your free tweaks, and any settings you had before using F1zz Tweaks, stay as they are.

Different plans

The same rules apply to the 30-day and the lifetime plan.

Premium Plus and Premium Ultimate

The rules above apply to these plans too. A guide that won't open or doesn't work for your console counts as something not working, so open a ticket within 7 days of buying with your order number. A refund or dispute switches your code off in both apps.

Getting a new PC

Codes are locked to one PC. If you replace your PC or reinstall Windows, open a ticket with your new Device ID and we'll help you move your code. No new purchase needed.

Please talk to us before disputing a payment

Most problems can be fixed quickly in a ticket. If a payment is disputed or reversed with your bank or card company, the license ends the same way as for a refund.

Privacy

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

The app

  • There are no accounts, and the app does not ask for or collect your email.
  • Your settings and the original values it backs up are stored on your own PC.
  • The change history (which tweaks were turned on or off, and when) and your saved profiles are stored only on your own PC and are never sent anywhere.
  • Hardware information (CPU, graphics, memory, displays) is read on your PC and is not sent anywhere.
  • The app goes online only to check for updates and to check whether a Premium license has been refunded.
  • To find your games, the app looks at where Steam, Epic, Riot and other launchers installed them. This stays on your PC.
  • If Discord is open on your PC, the app shows "Playing F1zz Tweaks" and the page you're on in your Discord status. You can turn this off in Settings.

F1zz Console Tweaks

The console app sends your code and Device ID to our order system to check your plan and load your guides. It saves your code, encrypted, and your progress on your PC. Your progress isn't sent to us.

Buying Premium

Payments are handled by the payment company shown at checkout. We never see your card details. The provider shares your order details with us, which can include the name and email you entered at checkout, so we can check your purchase and help you. We keep that information only as long as we need it for support and refunds.

Before checkout, the Premium page asks for your Device ID and your email. They are only used to make your code and email it to you. They are passed to our payment provider (Stripe) with your order, and our order system (run on Cloudflare) keeps a record of your email, Device ID, code and order so we can resend your code, handle refunds and switch off refunded codes. Codes are emailed through Resend. The website itself does not save your email. It only remembers your Device ID in your own browser so you don't have to type it again.

Discord tickets and codes

If you open a ticket we see your Discord username, your order number and your Device ID. We use them only to make your code and help you. We keep a record of the codes we issue (a code ID, the Device ID, the email or Discord name we sent it to, and the date) so we can handle refunds and support.
